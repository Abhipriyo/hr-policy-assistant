# Code of Conduct

Document HR-05 · Version 1.4 · Effective 1 July 2025

## 1. Our standards

Every employee is expected to act honestly, treat colleagues and clients with respect, and protect company and client information. This code applies at work, at company events, and when using company systems.

## 2. Gifts and hospitality

Employees may accept gifts from clients or vendors worth up to INR 2,000 in total from any one source in a year. Gifts above this value must be declined or handed over to the Compliance team. Cash, vouchers and gift cards must never be accepted, whatever their value.

## 3. Conflicts of interest

Any actual or possible conflict of interest must be declared to your manager and the Compliance team within 15 days of becoming aware of it. Examples include a family member working for a vendor, or a financial interest in a competitor.

Taking up paid work outside the company, including freelancing, needs prior written approval from HR.

## 4. Workplace harassment

The company has zero tolerance for sexual harassment. Complaints are handled by the Internal Committee under the POSH Act, and can be emailed to ic@brightwave.example. The Internal Committee completes its inquiry within 90 days of receiving a complaint.

Complaints should be made within 3 months of the incident. The Internal Committee may extend this by a further 3 months if there is a good reason for the delay.

## 5. Reporting concerns

Concerns about fraud, bribery or other unethical behaviour can be raised through the whistleblower hotline, which allows anonymous reports. The company does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## 6. Disciplinary process

Breaches of this code are handled through a progressive process: a verbal warning, then a written warning, then a final written warning, and then termination. Serious misconduct, such as fraud, violence or harassment, can lead directly to termination.
