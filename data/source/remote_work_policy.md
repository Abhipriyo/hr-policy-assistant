# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Document HR-02 · Version 2.0 · Effective 1 January 2026

## 1. Hybrid working

Brightwave Analytics operates a hybrid model. Employees work from the office 3 days a week, and Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are the default office days. Teams may agree different office days with their department head, as long as the 3-day minimum is met.

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST. During core hours, employees should be reachable on the company chat tool and available for meetings.

New employees work from the office 5 days a week for their first 4 weeks, to support onboarding.

## 2. Fully remote roles

A fully remote arrangement requires written approval from the department's Vice President and the HR Business Partner. Fully remote arrangements are reviewed every 12 months.

## 3. Allowances

Employees on a hybrid or remote arrangement receive an internet allowance of INR 1,500 per month, paid with salary.

Employees can claim a one-time home office setup reimbursement of up to INR 15,000 for a chair, desk or monitor. This reimbursement can be claimed again once every 3 years. Claims are submitted through the expense system with the original invoice.

## 4. Working from another location

Employees may work from another city in India for up to 4 weeks at a time, with their manager's approval.

Working from outside India is limited to 30 days in a calendar year. It needs prior approval from HR, because of tax and data-protection requirements. Employees must not access client data from countries on the company's restricted list.

## 5. Equipment and security

The company provides a laptop to every employee. Company data must only be stored on company-approved systems. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the company VPN switched on. A lost or stolen laptop must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours.
