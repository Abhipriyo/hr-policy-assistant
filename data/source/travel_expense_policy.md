# Travel and Expense Policy

Document FIN-04 · Version 4.1 · Effective 1 April 2026

## 1. Booking travel

All business travel must be booked through the company travel portal. Bookings made outside the portal are reimbursed only if the employee shows the fare was lower than the portal's fare for the same journey.

Trips must be approved by your reporting manager before booking. International travel also needs approval from the department's Vice President.

## 2. Air and rail travel

Domestic flights are booked in economy class for all employees. International flights are booked in economy class, except that employees at Director level and above may book business class for flights longer than 8 hours.

Rail travel up to AC 2-tier is allowed for journeys under 8 hours.

## 3. Hotels

Hotel stays are capped per night, including taxes:

- Metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune): INR 7,000 per night
- Other cities in India: INR 4,500 per night
- International: USD 180 per night

Stays above the cap need approval from the Finance Head before booking.

## 4. Daily allowance

A daily allowance covers meals and incidental expenses. It is INR 1,200 per day for domestic travel and USD 60 per day for international travel. No receipts are needed for the daily allowance. The daily allowance is not paid when meals are provided by a client or at a conference.

## 5. Local travel and mileage

Taxis and ride-hailing services are reimbursed at actual cost. Employees who use their own vehicle for business travel are reimbursed INR 12 per km for a car and INR 5 per km for a two-wheeler.

## 6. Submitting expense claims

Expense claims must be submitted in the expense system within 30 days of the end of the trip. Claims submitted later than 30 days need approval from the Finance Head and may be refused.

Original receipts are required for any single expense above INR 500.

Claims are approved by your reporting manager. Any single claim above INR 50,000 also needs approval from the Finance Head. Approved claims are paid with the next salary cycle.

## 7. Non-reimbursable expenses

The following are not reimbursed: alcohol, traffic fines and parking penalties, personal entertainment such as in-room movies, laundry for trips shorter than 4 nights, and costs for family members who travel with you.

## 8. Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than 6 times a year may apply for a corporate card. Corporate cards must only be used for business expenses, and personal use is a disciplinary matter.
