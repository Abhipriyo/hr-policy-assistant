# Probation, Confirmation and Exit Policy

Document HR-07 · Version 2.3 · Effective 1 April 2026

## 1. Probation

All new employees serve a probation period of 6 months. The manager may extend probation once, by up to 3 months, if more time is needed to assess performance. Any extension must be confirmed in writing before the original probation period ends.

At the end of probation, the employee receives a confirmation letter or a written extension.

## 2. Notice period

During probation, either the employee or the company may end employment with 15 days' notice.

After confirmation, the notice period is 60 days. Employees at level L5 and above have a notice period of 90 days.

## 3. Notice buyout and early release

An employee may ask to be released before the end of the notice period. Early release is at the discretion of the reporting manager and the HR Business Partner. If it is approved, the employee may buy out the remaining notice days at their basic salary rate.

Unused annual leave cannot be adjusted against the notice period.

## 4. Exit process

Resignations must be submitted through the HR portal. Every leaving employee is offered an exit interview with HR.

All company assets, including the laptop and ID card, must be returned on or before the last working day.

The full and final settlement is paid within 45 days of the last working day. A relieving letter and experience letter are issued after the settlement is complete.
